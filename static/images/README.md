# Imagenes originales del catalogo

Tres imagenes generadas con la herramienta integrada de generacion de imagenes, no descargadas de PuntoTicket ni copiadas de artistas reales. Son ilustraciones de **eventos ficticios de demostracion**, no fotografias documentales de conciertos anunciados.

Se usan localmente, sin depender de un servicio externo al navegar:

- `static/images/live.png`
- `static/images/electronic.png`
- `static/images/acoustic.png`

## Prompts utilizados

### live.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original fictional concert catalog artwork for Pulso Tickets, landscape 1536x1024. Subject: an indie rock band performing on a clearly visible festival stage at blue hour, wide frontal view from the crowd with guitars, drums, microphones and detailed light rigging. Teal, coral and warm white stage lights. Bright, crisp editorial concert photography, energetic but not blurry or dark. No recognizable real performers, no logos, no text, no watermarks. This is illustration for fictional demo events, not a real artist photo.

### electronic.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional electronic concert catalog artwork, landscape 1536x1024. A large modern outdoor electronic music stage with a female DJ behind equipment, seen clearly from the audience. Sculptural rectangular LED panels showing geometric coral-red and aqua graphics, white spotlights, lively festival crowd under a deep dusk sky. Crisp, bright, detailed editorial photograph with clear equipment and stage. No real artist likenesses, no logos, no letters, no watermark. Original illustrative image for fictional demo events.

### acoustic.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional acoustic concert catalog artwork landscape 1536x1024. Intimate contemporary theater performance: a Latin American woman playing acoustic guitar and singing at a microphone, with a double-bass player and a small percussion setup. Wide shot with the artists and instruments clearly visible. Elegant deep burgundy theater backdrop, warm-white spotlights, soft mint accent lights. Natural stage lighting, crisp realistic editorial photography. No recognizable real artists, no logos, no words, no watermark. This is original illustrative imagery for fictional demo events.
